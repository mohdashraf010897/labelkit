Open source · EU AI Act Article 50

Label every AI output your app generates.

Label Kit wraps your image, text and audio generation calls. It attaches C2PA and IPTC provenance, gives you a disclosure label, keeps an audit log, and checks it all in CI. One dependency, no vendor lock-in.

01 — Why now

The labelling rules are already in force.

2 Aug 2026Art. 50 applies

Providers must mark synthetic image, video, audio and text in a machine-readable, detectable way.

2 Dec 2026Grace period ends

For marking duties on systems already on the market before August.

3%of global turnover

Or €15M, whichever is higher. Applies to non-EU providers whose output reaches the EU.

Sources: Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, Art. 50 and Art. 99 · EC AI Act Service Desk · Code of Practice on AI-generated content.

03 — Honest scope

What it proves, and what it can't.

Does
  • Machine-checkable marking: manifest present, signature valid
  • One API across providers and media types
  • An audit trail you can show a regulator or a customer
  • Built on open standards (C2PA, IPTC)
Does not
  • Give legal advice or certify compliance
  • Decide your editorial-review exemption
  • Guarantee a watermark survives every edit
  • Phone home: everything runs in your own stack
